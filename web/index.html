finalgrade

Final grades from a Gradescope or Canvas export — category weights, dropped lowests, late penalties, per‑student waivers.

Your whole grading policy is one small file. Every weight, dropped lowest, late penalty and waiver you set here is written to a config.yaml you can download and keep — hand it to a TA, put it in your syllabus repo, or drop it back on this page next term and pick up where you left off. The command line tool reads the very same file, so nothing here is a dead end.

Your grades never leave this computer: it all runs inside this page. (Try disabling the network after it loads.)
