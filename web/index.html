finalgrade

Final grades from a Gradescope or Canvas export — category weights, dropped lowests, late penalties, per‑student waivers.

Your grades never leave this computer. The whole tool runs inside this page, so the csv you pick is never uploaded anywhere. You can turn off your network after this page loads.
